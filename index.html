Privacy Policy

Smart Calorie Calculator (package: com.smartcalorie.app)
Last updated: October 27, 2026

Smart Calorie Calculator ("the app", "we") is an Android app that estimates calories and nutrients from photos of food and helps you log meals and water. The app is developed by Pankaj Kumar ("the developer"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and what choices you have.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy it is used
Account information: your email address, and your name and profile picture if you sign in with GoogleTo create your account, sign you in, and keep your data linked to you
Profile and health-related details you enter: name, gender, age, height, weight, activity level, goal, and daily calorie, macro and water targetsTo calculate your daily targets and personalise the app
Food photos you take or choose from your galleryTo identify food and estimate calories and nutrients
Meal and water logs: meal names, calories, macros, ingredients, a small thumbnail of the photo, date and time, and water intakeTo show your diary and trends, and to sync your data across sign-ins
Usage limits: a count of AI scans per day linked to your accountTo prevent abuse and keep the service available

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, phone number, or advertising ID, and the app does not show ads.

2. Permissions the app uses

  • Camera: to take photos of food for analysis.
  • Photos / media access: to let you pick an existing food photo from your gallery.
  • Internet: to sign in, run the AI analysis and sync your data.

3. How your photos are processed

When you scan a food photo, the app sends it over an encrypted connection to our backend (hosted on Supabase). The backend forwards the image to Google's Gemini AI service to identify the food and estimate nutrition, and returns the result to the app. We do not store the full-size photo on our servers. A small thumbnail (about 200 pixels wide) of the photo may be saved with your meal log so you can recognise the meal later. Google processes the image under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase: authentication, database and server functions.
  • Google: Google Sign-In (if you choose it) and the Gemini AI service used to analyse food photos.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Data storage and security

Some data is stored on your device. When you are signed in, your profile and meal logs are also stored in our Supabase database, protected by access rules so that each account can only access its own data. Data is sent over HTTPS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data while your account is active. You can delete individual meals inside the app at any time, and you can sign out or uninstall the app to remove locally stored data.

To delete your account and all associated data held on our servers, email us at the address below from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete your data within 30 days.

7. Your choices

  • You can use parts of the app without entering personal details, but AI food scanning requires you to sign in.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your data by emailing us.
  • You can revoke camera and photo permissions at any time in your phone's settings.

8. Health information notice

The calorie and nutrient values are AI estimates and can be inaccurate. The app is for general information only and is not medical advice. Consult a qualified professional before making health or diet decisions.

9. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
